\subsubsection{Medical transfer: exact fitting restrictions}\label{app:robustness-transfer}

The primary seed-42 fitting pools contain 63 MedQA and 197 MedMCQA questions, with fixed target complements of 1,210 and 3,744. Equal source budgets use \texttt{estimation\_order[:63]} from the saved source partition. Thus the MedMCQA source subset is nested in its original randomized pool, with no target test question added. Target in-domain fits remain unchanged. The forward prior averages the same 63 MedQA questions. Its averaging order differs from the sorted primary list only by floating-point accumulation (at most $2.8\times10^{-16}$ per coordinate), with exactly identical predictions and endpoints. The reverse prior averages 63 rather than 197 MedMCQA questions.

For calibration-pool resampling, each benchmark and seed in $\{42,43,44,45,46\}$ initializes a separate Python \texttt{random.Random(seed)} generator. Starting from sorted original fit IDs, it draws 63 or 197 indices using \texttt{randrange(pool\_size)} with replacement. Each selected question contributes its normalized geometric prior once per occurrence. Both priors are refitted, and the same dataset-specific draw is reused across models and transfer directions. Repeated draws are not collapsed or deduplicated. The resulting unique counts range from 36 to 43 for MedQA and 121 to 126 for MedMCQA.

The original seed-42 primary fit is a reference only. The with-replacement seed-42 draw differs from it. A full-pool, without-replacement control at all five seeds reproduces all original prior arrays and all four methods\textquotesingle{} accuracy/RStd endpoints exactly. Its zero variability follows from exhausting the available pool and is not evidence that calibration is generally stable. The informative condition measures variation conditional on one empirical fitting pool and a fixed test set. Its sample SD uses \texttt{ddof=1}. It neither estimates uncertainty over all dataset splits nor replaces the primary paired question-bootstrap intervals.

Forward equal-budget endpoints are identical to Table~\ref{tab:transfer-medqa} by construction.

\FloatBarrier\subsubsection{Frozen-prior corrected cyclic endpoints}\label{app:robustness-permutation}

Calibration uses the first existing path-seeded draw, with source-path bytes seeding the sequential shuffle as in the primary hybrid protocol. Only its complement is evaluated. A single prior is applied as $\log(p+10^{-10})-\log(\pi+10^{-10})$ in displayed-label space at every shift, followed by first-maximum selection and the permanent-content mapping. Cycles are complete: four per item, or five for CSQA. The calibration sets have no overlap with these evaluation sets. Membership may differ between models and from the medical seed-42 transfer holdouts.

In Tables~\ref{tab:corrected-mmlu}--\ref{tab:corrected-medmcqa}, $A_0$ is original-order accuracy, $R_0$ original RStd, $R_{\rm cyc}$ cyclic RStd, $\bar A$ mean per-shift accuracy, and $S$ and range describe per-shift accuracy. $C$ is all-shift semantic consistency. Rows $D$ and $P$ use identical holdout questions. Row $P$ is pure frozen-prior PriDe, not the full-population hybrid. Accuracy/consistency are percent and all dispersion quantities are pp. All models retain the previously declared first-maximum and additive-epsilon conventions. CPA is not a per-shift accuracy average, and its deterministic complete-orbit invariance is an aggregation property.
